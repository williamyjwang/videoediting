MCC VISUAL IDENTITY SYSTEM / MCC 品牌视觉系统
Version 1.0 | 2026-10-19 | Status: PROPOSED — pending MCC approval
Owner: To assign | Source: MCC Sales Deck (6 pages), two recent Xiaohongshu Blue-V visual posts, and MCC brand positioning.
IMPORTANT: This document is a design proposal, not an approved current-facts or offer sheet. Existing MCC logo artwork must be used; text mockups are not official logo files. No student imagery or claims are approved by this document.

01 — BRAND FOUNDATION / 品牌定位
Direction: Modern Prestige / 现代精英质感.
Values: Premium (高端), Modern (现代), Confident (自信), Human (真实有温度).
English: A modern international coaching and personal development brand combining the sophistication of a private club with the approachability of a lifestyle brand.
中文：兼具国际精英社群的品质感、现代教育品牌的专业度与真实生活方式品牌的亲和力。
Visual principle: One Brand. One Visual Language. One Consistent Experience.
Reference positioning: Confidence. Connection. Presence. / 自信表达、真实连接、个人影响力.
Brand line appearing in deck: Build English into your life. Any new slogan requires approval.

02 — COLOR PALETTE / 色彩系统
MCC Black #0D0D0D — primary dark background / 主背景.
Signature Gold #C7A36B — accents, key words, rules, approved logo variants / 金色强调.
Warm Ivory #F5F2EB — light backgrounds, dark-mode main text / 暖白.
Graphite #242424 — secondary dark surface / 深灰.
Stone Gray #A7A29A — secondary text, neutral elements / 中性灰.
Espresso #302820 — warm atmospheric accent / 暖棕.
Indicative brand-wide composition: Black 55%, Ivory 25%, Gold 12%, Neutrals 8%. This is NOT a per-artwork requirement.
Gold is an accent, not a full-page default; avoid gold paragraphs, loud metallic gradients, and low-contrast small gold text on ivory.
For print: derive and proof CMYK/spot values with the printer; HEX values alone are not print specifications.

03 — TYPOGRAPHY / 字体
Exactly two everyday typeface families:
English: Inter.
Simplified Chinese: Noto Sans SC.
Display: Bold; headings: Semibold; body: Regular; labels: Medium; captions: Regular.
No arbitrary third fonts, faux bold, stretched lettering or inconsistent tracking.
Existing MCC logo is a separate vector artwork; never reconstruct its letterforms in Inter.
Editorial serif is NOT part of the everyday system. Exceptional campaign typography requires approval.
Starting size ranges (adjust after actual preview):
Desktop web H1 48–72 px, body 16–20 px.
Mobile web H1 32–44 px, body 16–18 px.
Xiaohongshu cover headline 60–100 px, supporting text 30–42 px at 1080 px width.
Vertical video subtitles 42–58 px at 1080 × 1920.
Sales deck titles 36–54 pt, body 18–24 pt.
Always prioritize legibility, mobile preview and accessible contrast.

04 — LOGO / 标志
Preserve original MCC logo geometry.
Approved-design candidate variants to prepare from official vector: Gold on Black; Ivory on Black; Black on Ivory; Monochrome.
Initial proposed clear space: at least 25% of logo height on each side; confirm against original vector.
Do not distort, recolor arbitrarily, add drop shadows, outlines or uncontrolled gradients.
Store master SVG/PDF and transparent PNG exports in the official brand asset library.

05 — PHOTOGRAPHY / 摄影
Art direction: Cinematic Realism / 电影感真实摄影.
Primary subjects: authentic conversations, international friendships, professional communication, everyday cafés, modern offices, real networking environments.
Look: neutral-warm color temperature, moderately strong contrast with preserved shadow detail, gently restrained saturation, realistic skin tones, soft natural or warm ambient light.
Avoid neon-blue/purple filters, over-retouching, staged luxury stereotypes and false representation of MCC students or events.
Use real licensed/consented photography wherever possible. AI concepts must not be represented as real students or MCC events.
Image categories: Social Connection; Professional Presence; Everyday English; Brand Atmosphere.

06 — XIAOHONGSHU / SOCIAL CONTENT
Standard portrait post: 1080 × 1440 (3:4). Check platform crop and safe areas.
Template A — Lifestyle Editorial / 生活方式：real photo + subtle dark gradient + ivory/gold title. Small talk, networking, social situations.
Template B — Educational Insight / 知识观点：black or graphite background, bold concise headline, gold emphasis. Communication, pronunciation, cross-cultural insights.
Template C — Institutional Framework / 品牌体系：ivory background, black copy, restrained gold numbers/rules. MCC methodology, frameworks, announcements.
All templates share the same official logo, Inter/Noto Sans SC, palette, spacing logic and CTA rules.
Do not imply small talk or networking are separate products unless formally approved.

07 — VIDEO DESIGN / 视频视觉
Vertical short-form: 1080 × 1920 (9:16). Horizontal video: 1920 × 1080 (16:9).
Logo in consistent corner with safe margins; never obscure faces.
Chinese subtitles: Noto Sans SC Medium/Semibold. English: Inter Medium/Semibold.
Subtitle foreground: Warm Ivory #F5F2EB; highlighted words: Signature Gold #C7A36B; use semi-transparent black backing where necessary.
Open: concise hook; end card: official MCC logo plus currently approved CTA.
Transitions: clean cuts, soft fades, restrained motion; avoid bouncing rainbow captions and excessive zooms.
Speaker and real demonstrations remain visually dominant.
Grading reference: slightly warm, mildly desaturated, moderate/high contrast; tune to camera and lighting rather than applying fixed settings indiscriminately.

08 — WEBSITE / 网站
Recommended section rhythm: Dark Hero → Light Content → Dark CTA.
Dark hero: brand line, concise value proposition, gold primary action.
Light content: ivory surfaces, black text, structured cards, readable body copy.
Dark CTA: focused conversion section with verified current enrollment action.
Primary button: Gold background #C7A36B with near-black text; secondary button: outlined/transparent.
Use restrained corner radii, thin neutral/gold dividers, consistent spacing and subtle fades.
Test WCAG contrast and mobile accessibility; gold small text on ivory is discouraged.
Web UI should not be all-black: alternating surfaces improve sustained reading.

09 — SALES DECK / MARKETING COLLATERAL
Three standardized slide families:
Cover — Black background, strong ivory heading, minimal gold accent.
Content — Ivory background, black body text, gold section accents and readable charts.
Section Divider — Graphite/Black, large section number, clear heading.
Apply consistently to sales decks, course guides, WeChat long-form images, downloadable PDFs and event collateral.
The six-page supplied sales deck is a visual reference, NOT authoritative verification of duration, coaching inclusions or outcomes.
